# Pricing research synthesis

## When to use

- You have pricing interviews, a price survey, competitor pricing pages, or conversion numbers, and no single conclusion from them.
- You are about to publish a first price or change one, and want the evidence behind the number.
- You need to know which pricing questions your data can't answer yet.

## Steps

1. Read [`INDEX.md`](../../../.productmap/INDEX.md), [`business-model.md`](../../../.productmap/01_strategy/business-model/business-model.md), [`unit-economics.md`](../../../.productmap/03_analysis/unit-economics/unit-economics.md), and all pricing inputs under [`06_data/`](../../../.productmap/06_data/): interviews, survey results, saved pricing pages, conversion exports.
2. Derive the acceptable price range and show the method, the sample, and the segment. Grade each input as stated, anchored, committed, or paid.
3. Gather the evidence for the value metric, recommend tiers, and list the risks and the next pricing tests.
4. Save the synthesis as `.productmap/01_strategy/business-model/pricing-research-YYYY-MM-DD.md` and write a summary into the Revenue Streams row of the Lean Canvas.
