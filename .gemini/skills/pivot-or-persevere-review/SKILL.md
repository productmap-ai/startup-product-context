# Pivot or persevere review

## When to use

- A round of experiments has finished and you need to decide what the results mean together.
- Growth or retention is flat and the team disagrees on whether to keep going.
- You are about to spend another quarter of runway on the same plan.

## Steps

1. Read [`INDEX.md`](../../../.productmap/INDEX.md), [`experiments.md`](../../../.productmap/02_discovery/experiments/experiments.md), [`pmf-signals.md`](../../../.productmap/01_strategy/product-market-fit/pmf-signals.md), [`metrics-tracking.md`](../../../.productmap/03_analysis/kpis-metrics/metrics-tracking.md), and all files in [`06_data/interviews/`](../../../.productmap/06_data/interviews/) and [`06_data/feedback/`](../../../.productmap/06_data/feedback/).
2. Give each assumption a verdict of validated, invalidated, or inconclusive against the success criterion written before the test ran.
3. Lay out the options: persevere, narrow the segment, change channel, change the problem, or pause. Show the evidence for and against each.
4. Save the decision memo with a 30-day plan as `.productmap/02_discovery/experiments/pivot-or-persevere-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
